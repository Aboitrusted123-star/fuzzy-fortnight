# PeekScan Brand Identity

## Brand Essence
PeekScan is a tool for warehouse and logistics workers who need speed, reliability, and zero friction. The brand is **industrial, confident, and sharp** — like a tool you grab without thinking.

## Logo Concept
The logo combines:
- A stylized **eye** (representing "peek" / seeing through)
- A **scan line** sweeping across a barcode silhouette (representing "scan")
- The **PeekScan** wordmark in a bold, sans-serif industrial typeface

### Symbol
A simplified eye icon with a rectangular pupil that doubles as a barcode scanner beam — the beam cuts through a faint diagonal stripe (representing "seeing through tape/seal"). The whole mark sits inside a rounded square with a subtle border, evoking a camera viewfinder.

### Colors
- **Primary mark:** Bright Cyan (#00D4FF) on dark background
- **Alternative:** White on dark backgrounds, dark slate on light

## Tagline Options
1. "See through it."
2. "Scan through anything."
3. "No peel. No delay."

## Brand Voice
- Direct, minimal, functional
- No jargon, no fluff
- Speaks like a warehouse worker, not a marketer
