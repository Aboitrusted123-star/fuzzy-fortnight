## Design Specs
- **Background:** Dark (#0D0D0D)
- **Surface Cards:** #1A1A1A with 1px border #262626
- **Primary Accent:** Cyan #00D4FF for buttons and highlights
- **Text:** White (#FFFFFF) for headings, #B3B3B3 for body
- **Font:** Inter (system sans-serif)
- **Hero CTA Button:** Cyan fill #00D4FF, dark text #0D0D0D, rounded 8px, 16px padding
- **Max content width:** 1200px, centered
- **Responsive:** Single column below 768px

## Navigation Items
1. Features (scroll to)
2. Pricing (scroll to)
3. Blog (external link)
4. Download (CTA button)

- Hero: "See through it."
