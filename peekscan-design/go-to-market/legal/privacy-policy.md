# PeekScan Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 3, 2026

## 1. Introduction

PeekScan ("we," "our," or "us") is a barcode and QR code scanning application that reads codes through seals, tape, stickers, and shrink wrap. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Camera Data
- The App requires camera access to scan barcodes and QR codes.
- **No images are uploaded or stored on our servers.** All camera processing happens locally on your device.
- Scanned barcode values may be saved locally to your device's scan history.

### 2.2 Usage Data
- App usage statistics (number of scans, feature usage) may be collected anonymously for product improvement.
- Crash reports and performance data (via Apple/Google standard SDKs).

### 2.3 Account Data (Enterprise Users)
- Enterprise account holders: name, email address, company name.
- Payment information is processed by Apple App Store / Google Play — we never store payment details.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
- To provide and maintain the scanning service
- To improve scan accuracy and app performance
- To communicate about updates, support, and enterprise billing
- To comply with legal obligations

## 4. Data Storage & Security
- Scanned barcodes are stored **only on your device** in local app storage.
- Enterprise configuration data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
- We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data.

## 5. Third-Party Services
- **Apple App Store / Google Play:** Payment processing, app distribution.
- **RevenueCat (optional):** Subscription/purchase management.
- We do not sell your data to third parties.

## 6. Your Rights
- Access and delete your scan history at any time from within the App.
- Disable camera access via device settings (App will not function).
- Request deletion of enterprise account data by contacting support.

## 7. Children's Privacy
The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy. Users will be notified of material changes via the App or email.
