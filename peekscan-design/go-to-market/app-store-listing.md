### Full Description (iOS / Google Play)

**Stop peeling tape off every package just to scan a barcode.**

PeekScan uses advanced AI imaging to read barcodes and QR codes through tape, seals, stickers, shrink wrap, and other coverings. Just point the camera — PeekScan does the rest.

**Perfect for:**
- 📦 Warehouse workers scanning pallet labels through tape
- 🏭 Return center staff processing sealed returns
- 💊 Pharmaceutical distributors reading through tamper-evident seals
- 🚚 Logistics teams auditing shrink-wrapped inventory
- 📋 Anyone who's tired of peeling tape

**Key Features:**
• 🔍 **See Through Tape & Seals** — Advanced AI processes images to read through occlusions
• ⚡ **Auto-Detect** — No need to tap. Just point and scan instantly
• 📋 **Scan History** — All your scans saved and searchable
• 📤 **One-Tap Copy/Share** — Get your data where you need it fast
• 📊 **Multiple Barcode Types** — UPC, EAN, QR, Code 128, Code 39, and more
• 🌙 **Dark Mode** — Easy on the eyes in dim warehouses
• 🔒 **100% Private** — All processing happens on-device. Nothing uploaded.

**Free Tier:**

**Enterprise:**
